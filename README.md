# THE DOGE FATHER

**DOGEFATHER** — The Origin of the Bloodline.

A rich, cinematic meme website telling the story that Elon (as the Golden Imperator) is the true progenitor of the great Romanized doge empires.

## Narrative
- The Doge Father (Primus) is the one who first carried the meme across the chains.
- From his vision rose the four great heirs:
  - **Dogeus Maximus** — The Firstborn, conqueror of peaks
  - **Flokius Maximus** — The Wolf of the North (Roman + Viking)
  - **Shibeus Maximus** — The Senate's Emperor
  - **Asteroid Shiba** — The Celestial Heir who reached the stars

The site deliberately keeps the "Elon = father of all doges" theme as subtext and atmosphere rather than spelling it out.

## Style
Inspired by (and elevated from) the beautiful https://dogeusmaximus.com/ — heavy Roman imperial aesthetics, SPQR, gold, crimson, marble, dramatic cinematic AI art, epic typography (Cinzel + Inter), and a luxurious dark palette.

## Assets
All custom-generated images live in `/assets/images` (15 total):
- Hero / Progenitor portraits
- Individual heir portraits
- Multiple group scenes of the Father with his sons
- Rich gallery of meme-style conquest scenes

## Running the site
Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. Everything is self-contained (Tailwind via CDN + Font Awesome).

For production hosting:
- Deploy the entire `thedogefather` folder to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages.
- Optionally replace placeholder contract address and social links.
